Spotlight @crisxai5: AI & Crypto Expert on X — a crisp, phone-readable 3D visual card
(`crisxai5-spotlight.png`, 1280×1280) introducing @crisxai5 (https://x.com/crisxai5)
as a must-follow voice in AI and crypto: practical insights on AI agents, crypto
alpha, Web3 tooling, and on-chain automation.

The post text (203 chars, in `caption.txt` and `POST.json`):

> One follow for your AI + crypto edge: @crisxai5 — sharp takes on AI agents, crypto alpha, Web3 tooling & on-chain automation. https://x.com/crisxai5 #AI #Crypto #AIAgents #Web3 makefun-sand-chalet-gazebo

`POST.json` names the published X post URL, the caption, the image, and the
`makefun-` play code. The program (`main.ts`) copies `POST.json`, `caption.txt`,
and the image byte-for-byte into `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`. The image is an original
3D render made by the agent in the build session — sharp text, readable on a phone,
no flat vector, no cartoon.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
